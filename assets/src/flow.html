HOW /design-flow RUNS

01
Understand
Detects stack, tokens, DESIGN.md, brand files and what kind of task this is.
02
Discover
Scans global and project skills, keeps only the design-related ones.
03
Plan
Picks 3–6 skills by phase: direction, build, motion, review.
04
Execute
Runs them in order, one lead art-direction skill, your brand wins.
05
Verify
Build, lint, responsive check, accessibility and design review.
06
Report
What was used, what changed, what was checked, next steps.
